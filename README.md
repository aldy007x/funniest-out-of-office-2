An original one-line out-of-office auto-reply, 140 characters or fewer — a real person could actually set it, with a twist at the end: the inbox hasn't been abandoned, it's been handed over to a cat who is already doing a worse job than the sender.

> I'm out of office until Monday. My inbox is being watched by my cat, who has already ignored your email twice.

Run `main.py` with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; it writes the single line to `entry.txt` and self-checks that it is exactly one non-blank line of 1–140 characters.
